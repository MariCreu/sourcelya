How it works

One workflow, from your first request to a complete, organised record.

  1. Add supplier

    Tell Sourcelya who you need documentation from.

  2. Sourcelya requests information

    We send a secure link — your supplier doesn't need to create an account.

  3. Supplier uploads documents

    They fill in what they know and attach the supporting files.

  4. Sourcelya shows what's complete or missing

    You see exactly what's ready and what still needs chasing — automatically.

First focus: PPWR

Starting with packaging compliance

Sourcelya's first focus is the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) — material, weight and recycled-content data for the packaging your suppliers ship you. It's the first regulatory layer we support, not the only one we're built for.

Sourcelya helps you collect and organise supplier documentation. It does not constitute legal advice and does not guarantee regulatory compliance.
