How it works

One workflow, from your first request to a complete, organised record.

  1. Add your supplier

    Tell Sourcelya which products or information you need.

  2. Sourcelya requests the information

    Your supplier gets a secure link. No account required.

  3. Your supplier uploads documents

    They can fill in the data and attach documents directly.

  4. Sourcelya shows what's missing

    It centralises the documentation and flags what's still pending.
