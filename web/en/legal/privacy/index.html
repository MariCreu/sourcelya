2. What data we process

Depending on how you use Sourcelya, we process:

  • Account data (customer company users): email and password, managed by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) — we never store your password in plaintext ourselves.
  • Customer company data: name and country entered at signup.
  • Supplier contact data: the supplying company's name and the email address the customer sends a request to. This is entered by the customer, not directly by the supplier.
  • Documents uploaded by suppliers: files a supplier attaches through the request link (technical datasheets, certificates, etc.), which may contain incidental personal data (e.g. a signatory's name on a certificate).
  • Audit and activity logs: what actions were taken on a request and when, for traceability.
  • Technical data: IP address and request metadata, kept briefly for security purposes (rate limiting, abuse prevention).

This public site (sourcelya.com) does not use cookies or store identifiers in your browser.

3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the service the customer company contracted for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6.1.b).
  • To contact the supplier to request documentation on the customer company's behalf (the customer company's legitimate interest in meeting its own compliance obligations toward its suppliers, GDPR Art. 6.1.f).
  • Security, fraud prevention and legal compliance.
  • Essential service communications (account confirmation, reminders about a pending request).
